Official Tournament Rules & Venue

Official handbook for the 2026 Summer Edition Road to the Final.

Official Venue

City Sports Complex

123 Stadium Drive, Cape Town, 8001 • Pitches A, B & C

⚽ 1. Tournament Format & Roster Structure

Match Format: Games are played as 5-a-side (4 outfield players + 1 goalkeeper).

Roster Size: Each team may register a maximum of 7 to 8 players total. Only registered roster players are eligible to play or substitute.

Substitutions: Rolling substitutions are permitted at any point during the match.

🏆 2. Competition Progression

Phase 1 (Group Stage): Teams will first compete in round-robin group play (3 group games across Groups A – H).

Phase 2 (Knockout Stage): Top-qualifying teams advance to single-elimination knockout rounds, progressing through to the final.

⏱️ 3. Punctuality & Forfeits

Late Arrival Penalty: If a team is not ready at the scheduled start time, a penalty goal will be awarded to the opposing team for every 2 minutes of delay.

Match Forfeit: If a team fails to field their squad within 5 minutes of the scheduled start time, the match is officially cancelled, and the tardy team automatically forfeits the match.

🟨 4. On-Pitch Rules & Offenses

No Slide Tackling: Slide tackles are strictly prohibited.

Sin-Bin Penalty: Infractions result in an immediate 5-minute yellow card / sin-bin penalty (the offending player leaves the pitch, and their team plays a man down for 5 minutes).

No Tackling from Behind: Marking or tackling directly from behind is strictly forbidden.

Zero Tolerance for Fighting: Any physical altercations or violent behavior will result in immediate team disqualification from the tournament.

💳 5. Payment & Refund Policy

Strict No Refund Policy: Registration fees are strictly non-refundable once transferred.

7-Day Cut-Off: No refund requests will be accepted or processed within 7 days prior to the tournament start date.
