The Road to the Final

Group stage first, then straight knockout football.

  1. 01

    Group stage

    32 teams in 8 groups of 4. Every team plays 3 matches.

  2. 02

    Round of 16

    The top 2 teams from each group qualify.

  3. 03

    Quarter-finals

    8 teams compete for 4 places.

  4. 04

    Semi-finals

    4 teams compete for a place in the final.

  5. 05

    Third-place playoff

    The semi-final losers play for third.

  6. 06

    The Final

    The last 2 teams compete for the championship.

Group points: win 3 • draw 1 • loss 0. Ties are split by goal difference, then goals scored.

What is the tournament format?

32 teams in 8 groups of 4, and every team plays 3 matches. The top 2 from each group go through to the Round of 16, then the quarter-finals, semi-finals, a third-place playoff and the final.

How long are matches?

20 minutes in total: 9 minutes, a 2-minute break, then another 9 minutes. Five players per side are on the pitch with rolling substitutes, across 3 pitches.

How do group points work?

Win = 3 points, draw = 1, loss = 0. Teams level on points are separated by goal difference, then goals scored.

Who can play?

Players must be 14 or older. Each team registers 5 to 8 players.

Where and when is it?

Saturday 07 November at Edenvale Indoor Soccer, Edenvale. The venue is open 09:00 to 20:00 with kick-off at 12:00, and awards and pack-down wrap up by 20:00.

What can we win?

R5,000 for 1st place, R2,500 for 2nd and R1,000 for 3rd, plus individual awards: Golden Boot, Player of the Tournament and Best Goalkeeper.
