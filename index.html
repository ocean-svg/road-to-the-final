HOW IT WORKS

Group stage, then knockout

Eight teams split into two groups of four. Everyone plays a round robin, then the top two from each group carry on to the knockout rounds — the road to the final.

01

Group stage

Round robin within Group A and Group B. Win = 3 points, draw = 1, loss = 0. Ranked by points, then goal difference.

02

Semi-finals

Group A winner faces Group B runner-up, and Group B winner faces Group A runner-up. Straight knockout, extra time if level.

03

The Final

Winners meet for the trophy. Level after extra time goes to penalties. Third-place teams receive a runners-up medal.

FORMAT DETAILS

Rules at a glance

On the pitch

  • 5 players per side, rolling substitutions, max 8 on the roster.
  • 2 x 20-minute halves in the group stage; 2 x 15 minutes plus extra time in the knockouts.
  • No offside. Goalkeeper cannot receive a back-pass by hand.
  • Slide tackles are not permitted — direct free kick and possible caution.

Competition rules

  • Teams must have 4 players present to kick off; a 10-minute grace period applies.
  • Group ties broken by: points → goal difference → goals scored → head-to-head.
  • Two yellow cards = one match suspension for the next fixture.
  • Registration closes one week before the group draw.
