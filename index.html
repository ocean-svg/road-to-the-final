Official Tournament Rules & Venue

Official handbook for the 2026 Summer Edition Road to the Final.

Official Venue

City Sports Complex

123 Stadium Drive, Cape Town, 8001 • Pitches A, B & C

⏱️ Match Format

• Two 20-minute halves per match (40 mins total).

• 5-minute half-time interval.

• Knockout ties proceed straight to penalties (5 kicks).

👥 Squad & Rolling Subs

• Maximum squad size: 12 registered players.

• Rolling substitutions permitted in Game 1, 2, 3.

• 5 max substitutions per team in knockout matches.

🏆 8 Groups Progression

• Exactly 3 group games per team (Game 1, 2, 3) across 8 groups (A to H).

• Win = 3 pts, Draw = 1 pt, Loss = 0 pts.

• Top 2 teams from each of the 8 groups advance to Round of 16.

🟨 Discipline

• Yellow Card: Warning (2 in a match = Red Card).

• Red Card: Immediate ejection + 1 match suspension.

• Yellow cards reset after the 3 Group Stage Games.
