Got Questions? We’ve Got Answers.

From sign-up to match day — what players need to know.

How do I register my team?

Tap Sign Up Team, add 5 to 8 players and send your squad through WhatsApp. Entry is R100 per player.

What is the tournament format?

32 teams in 8 groups. Every team plays 3 guaranteed group games of 10 minutes each, then the knockout stage follows. Matches run from 12:00 to 18:15 across 3 pitches.

Who can play?

Players must be 14 or older. Squads are 5 to 8 players, with 1 goalkeeper and 4 outfield players on the pitch.

Where and when is it?

Edenvale Indoor Soccer, Edenvale. The venue is open 09:00 to 20:00 and the tournament runs 12:00 to 18:15.

Are there prizes?

Yes — R5,000 for 1st place, R2,500 for 2nd and R1,000 for 3rd.
